Real speech test

The accuracy on the results page comes from written transcripts. This page measures real speech: a person reads 12 visits aloud, and the words the speech engine writes down are scored against the right answers.

  1. Pick the engine. Wispr Flow: click in the box and dictate with your Wispr Flow shortcut. Browser mic: tap the mic (Chrome or Edge).
  2. Read the sentence aloud at a normal pace, the way you'd tell a nurse. Don't fix what the engine writes.
  3. Press Next. At the end, download the results file.
