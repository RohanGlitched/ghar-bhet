Rules and method

How a spoken visit becomes a checked record, and every rule that can raise a flag, with its source. Nothing here uses an AI model.

From speech to a checked record

1. Speech to textThe browser's speech recognition, in Hindi (hi-IN), Marathi (mr-IN) or Indian English. Or she types.
2. Reading numbersDigits, number words in three languages, fractions (dedh, saadhe, paune, deed, adeech), "one forty", Marathi "deedshe", grams and kilos together.
3. Filling fieldsEach field has anchor words (wajan, BP, saans…); the value near it is taken, and the words are kept as evidence.
4. Signs and negationPhrases for each sign in three languages; "nahin", "naahi", "no" flip them to absent. The last value said wins, so corrections work.

Data and privacy

  • Visits are kept on the server that runs the app (one JSON file). No visit is sent to any other service.
  • Speech recognition is the browser's own; in Chrome it is processed by Google's speech service. The text, not the audio, reaches Ghar Bhet.
  • No audio is stored. The register keeps a first name, the visit fields and the transcript.
  • The live demo uses made-up sample people. A pilot would need consent and the state's data rules.
