Speak a home visit

You are the village health worker (ASHA). Tap the mic and say the visit.

?How to speak
  • Pick who you visited and your language, tap the mic, and say what you found, the way you would tell the nurse: name, age, weight, temperature, breathing, feeding, any danger sign.
  • The phone asks for what is missing, out loud, and opens the mic for your answer. "Haan" and "nahi" work, and so do "chhodo" (skip) and "pata nahi" (not checked).
  • Say "save karo" to save: the phone reads the record back and saves on "haan". "Padh ke sunao" reads it back; "bas" stops the mic.
  • Say "ruko" or "ek minute" while you measure: the mic waits up to 2 minutes. Say "bas" to stop the mic; say it twice to stop the questions.
  • Works in Chrome or Edge. Anywhere else, or with no internet, type it below, or dictate into the box with Wispr Flow or your keyboard's mic, or try an example.

What happens to your words
  1. Numbers can be words. "dedh sau" (150), "saadhe aath" (8.5), "ek kilo saat sau gram" (1.7 kg).
  2. Every value keeps the words it came from: tap a value to light them up. If nothing matches, the field stays empty and the phone asks for it.
  3. Danger signs come from fixed rules, the ones in the Health Ministry (MoHFW) manuals and the 2026 newborn guidelines, never from a model.
